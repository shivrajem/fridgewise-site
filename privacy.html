FridgeWise privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

FridgeWise does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

  • Where your data is stored. Everything you enter (items, prices, dates, shopping lists, settings) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to a server.
  • No accounts, analytics, ads or trackers. The app makes no network requests apart from loading its own files.
  • Notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push service is used.
  • Exports. CSV and backup files are created on your device. You decide where they go.
  • Deleting your data. Use Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Children. The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

If a future version adds optional cloud sync, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and sync will be opt-in.
